A few answers before you begin.

How much does it cost?

US$1, paid once. No recurring subscription.

How does the personal license work?

After payment is manually confirmed, you receive the extension and a personal serial or license file. It never expires and is not tied to a computer. Do not share your license.

Which versions are supported?

The developer lists SketchUp 2024, 2025 and 2026 for Windows. Future SketchUp or Qt changes may require an update.

Will it change my model?

It changes interface and viewport appearance, not geometry. The source restores original viewport settings before saving and reapplies dark mode afterward. This behavior still needs validation in SketchUp.

Does every window become dark?

Coverage varies. Third-party and system dialogs may retain their colors.

How do I return to light mode?

Use Disable / Light Mode in the extension menu, or toggle with the moon button. Disable the theme before uninstalling to restore the active viewport.

Is this an official Trimble product?

No. This is an independent extension, not affiliated with or endorsed by Trimble or SketchUp.
